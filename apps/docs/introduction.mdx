## What is Huddle?

Huddle is a tool for creating scheduling polls. It's designed to help people find a common time to schedule a meeting or event.

> Huddle is pronounced like "rally" but with an extra _L_.
>
> I wanted to call the product "Rally" because that's what it's for - rallying people together to make things happen.
> But, of course, the domain name was already taken. So, I did what any sane person would do - I added an extra _L_.
>
> -- [Areal](https://twitter.com/imareal), Creator of Huddle

## What is a Scheduling Poll?

Scheduling polls helps you find a time that works for everyone. The host
of the meeting will typically select a few date and time options for participants to vote
for and then select the most suitable option.
